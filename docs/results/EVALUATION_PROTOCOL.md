## Calendar target and support gates

Weekly target is complete SNPP operating-week truth compared with weekly estimates. Monthly target is exactly `calendar_month_daily_allocation`: allocate weekly estimates to daily values by the served Terra/Aqua daily-count proportions (zero-count weeks follow the implemented Monday allocation), sum daily SNPP truth and estimates by calendar month, and include only fully covered months. Test leap days and month/year boundaries. A report missing `month_target=calendar_month_daily_allocation`, using Monday-week buckets, or lacking compatible producer/input hashes is `incompatible`, never accepted.

Primary interval groups, each reported separately, are week/month × `terra_aqua`/`terra_only`; positive activity; positive truth with zero prediction; zero activity; magnitude; season; land cover; and area-size/support class. Always include sample count, unique contributing cells, unique spatial blocks, unique calendar years, interval coverage, both miss tails, mean width, interval score, and sparse-fallback count. Truth-defined groups are required diagnostics only, not deployable strata.

Minimum support for a group to be eligible for acceptance: **n ≥ 500**, at least **5 distinct 2-degree spatial blocks**, and at least **5 distinct calendar-year clusters**. For supported groups, use a **two-way pigeonhole cluster bootstrap** over spatial block and calendar year (2,000 replicates; seed **5301**) to form a 95% interval for coverage. Report the resampling method and unique cluster counts; repeated rows/rectangles are not independent Bernoulli trials. A group failing any support minimum is `insufficient_support`, not silently pooled. Preserve the existing severe stop: any reported group with n ≥ 500 and coverage < 0.80 is a `stop_condition`.

Acceptance requires point coverage in **[0.85, 0.95]** and the block/year bootstrap 95% interval to lie entirely in the same band, no severe stop, finite metrics, no unexplained exclusions, and no material width/interval-score regression against the frozen baseline on identical eligible samples. Width and score have no fixed numeric tolerance in the current record; before candidate evaluation, T54 must freeze a diagnosis-based noninferiority tolerance or explicitly mark this criterion unresolved and restrict the conclusion to descriptive development evidence. Overall marginal coverage never overrides group failure. Retain B1 eligibility; do not make the learned candidate win by rule.
