## MCD64A1 corroboration sample (predeclared for T33/T24)

- **Question:** do annual timing and relative seasonal activity from observed VIIRS active-fire detections align with a separate burned-area product in two supported administrative areas? This is corroboration only; MCD64A1 shares MODIS active-fire inputs and is not independent ground truth.
- **Collection:** NASA LP DAAC `MCD64A1.061`, requested from CMR provider `LPCLOUD`; cite the [MCD64A1 v061 User Guide](https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/1006/MCD64_User_Guide_V61.pdf) and [CMR Search API](https://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/site/docs/search/api.html).
- **Areas:** Dhaka district (`32408957B77718829177955`; bbox `89.25526666292177,22.833504172682865,91.25687007700935,24.791575504241223`) and Punjab state, India (`1811400B91946361694107`; bbox `73.87984832119756,29.54207982547574,76.93941792731886,32.51275197047561`) from the checked-in geoBoundaries ADM1 catalog in `ws5_frontend/public/demo/areas.json`. Spatial masking in the later analysis uses each full boundary polygon; CMR search boxes only identify candidate tiles.
- **Years/window:** full calendar years 2013, 2017 and 2021 for both areas, fixed before examining MCD64A1 agreement. These are evenly spaced checks within the shared VIIRS S-NPP/MODIS era; no replacement year is allowed based on observed agreement. All 12 monthly granules are requested per intersecting tile so annual activity and median timing are not clipped to a chosen season.
- **Budget:** at most 4 GiB total downloaded/stored across the six area-year requests. Run each of the six area-year invocations with `--max-bytes 715827882` (about 683 MiB), so their combined per-invocation caps stay below 4 GiB. Inspect `--inventory-only` first, and stop without downloading if the complete declared sample exceeds the total cap or any requested granule size is unknown. No widening of years or areas without a new predeclared protocol.
- **Roles and acceptance:** VIIRS detections supply the timing/activity comparator; MCD64A1 supplies monthly burned-pixel timing/area records. T24 must report all six area-year results, missing coverage, valid-pixel denominators, QA exclusions, disagreement and limitations. No minimum positive association is required, and burned area must never be converted into a detection count.
- **Outputs:** raw HDF granules and `mcd64a1-manifest.jsonl` remain local under `data/raw/` and are excluded from Git. This hand-off manifest records checked-in inputs; per-file source IDs, byte counts and SHA256 values are in the local JSONL manifest.
- **Acquisition result:** CMR inventory and local files reconciled for all 108 granules (Dhaka: 36; Punjab: 72), across `h26v06`, `h24v05` and `h24v06`. The 108 HDF4 signatures, catalog sizes and local SHA256 values all passed; 451,656,759 raw bytes; no missing, unexpected or partial files. Run receipt and the detailed file hashes are in the ignored local `data/raw/` directory. Transient CMR/TLS resets were resumed without changing the sample.
